Our story

The Vintage Cafe brings together a rustic room, a welcoming counter, and a menu built for both quick stops and longer sits.

01

A cozy roadside stop

Located on Hurontario Street in Caledon Village, the cafe is easy to pull into and easy to stay in.

02

Food and drinks with range

From sandwiches and wraps to chai, matcha, hojicha, espresso drinks, and iced lattes, the menu is built for everyday cravings.

03

A room with character

Dark ceiling beams, warm tables, large windows, and a chalkboard menu give the cafe its lived-in vintage feeling.
